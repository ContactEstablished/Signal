## Approved decisions — September 13, 2026

| ID | Approved decision | User-visible consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 — Today and week boundaries | Use Monday–Sunday weeks in the workspace timezone. Today follows the workspace clock, including its explicit September test clock. Overdue/Due today/Tomorrow exclude Done tasks; overdue means a due date before today, while an earlier time today stays in Due today. The seven-day strip and Week can show Done tasks struck through. Hours this week sums completed time entries by their local start date, as M3 Logged does; active timers remain separate. | Consistent calendar buckets and totals across views, without treating a missed time today as yesterday's task. |
| D2 — Week task dragging | Change only the due date. Preserve the existing local due time, including seconds, in the workspace timezone. A task with no deadline gets a visible, editable 17:00 default in a confirmation editor. A nonexistent or repeated destination time opens that editor to resolve it; never silently shift or choose an offset. Include a keyboard Move to date action. A No date destination explicitly clears the deadline; the Later section opens a date picker rather than guessing a day. | Moving a 15:30 task to Friday keeps 15:30. Task status, timer, logged time and existing planner blocks are unchanged. |
| D3 — Meeting editor | Meeting belongs to one project. Require a title, local start date/time with a visible IANA timezone, and elapsed duration of 1–1440 whole minutes. Offer 30m/45m/1h plus custom duration; allow overnight meetings. Default to the workspace timezone, the next 15-minute boundary, 30 minutes, no recurrence, a 15-minute reminder, and Show in Your Day ON. Support a safe HTTP(S) join link, agenda and Markdown notes. Show all defaults before Save. | One-off meetings have explicit instants and can span midnight. Reminder preferences are saved now; delivery remains M6. |
| D4 — Recurrence and DST | Daily means every calendar day; weekly means the same weekday. Keep the series at its wall-clock time in its saved timezone even if the workspace timezone changes. Offer Never/On date as the series end (inclusive local date). Skip a recurring slot in a spring-forward gap and explain the skipped date; for a repeated hour, expose Earlier/Later as a series choice, default Earlier. One-off or individually edited ambiguous starts still require explicit offset resolution. Project occurrences only for requested date ranges; do not prepopulate an unlimited future or create planner blocks. | A weekly 09:00 meeting stays at 09:00 in its chosen timezone through DST. Today, Week, Board and Your Day show the same occurrences. |
| D5 — Occurrence versus series changes | Recurring edit offers This occurrence or This and following; past occurrences, notes and attendance remain intact. Individually edited future occurrences keep their overrides and are listed in the change preview. Recurring removal cancels This occurrence or This and following using durable exclusions/cutoff, so refresh cannot recreate them. One-off deletion uses an affected-record confirmation and removes meeting-owned data/links only; linked tasks, planner task blocks and time entries survive. Project deletion retains its approved permanent-deletion semantics. | Editing next week's meeting does not rewrite a meeting already attended. No full calendar sync, arbitrary RRULE editor, or undo system is added. |
| D6 — Attendance and notes | Each occurrence has Unmarked/Attended/Missed, default Unmarked, changed manually in meeting detail. Joining or passing the end time does not infer attendance. Keep occurrence notes separate from shared series agenda/notes, and preserve them across rescheduling. Task detail links to meeting detail and exposes related meeting notes there; do not build a general task activity feed in M4. | M5 can use explicitly attended occurrences and their actual start dates without guessing historical attendance. |
| D7 — Visibility and linked tasks | Show in Your Day controls only planner display, occupancy and planned totals. Hidden occurrences remain in Today, project Week, Board and linked-task details. Allow linking any existing task, including another project's task, with the project name visible; the meeting keeps its owning project's color. Links default from the series with per-occurrence overrides. Every read consumer honors cancellation and uses the same stable occurrence identity. | Hiding a meeting from the planner also frees that planner slot; linking a task does not move it to another project or create a block. |
| D8 — Mock and milestone limits | The dated subtask in the Week reference is display-only mock content; do not add subtask deadlines. Store recurrence/attendance/visibility metadata additively, preserve existing UTC instants and seed edits, and assign legacy meetings the workspace timezone on migration without shifting them. Existing repeat metadata begins expanding in M4. Keep Notifications delivery in M6, summaries in M5, global palette/polish in M7, and assisted screenshot/text intake in proposed M8. | M4 delivers Today, Week and meeting workflows without unrelated schema or provider integrations. |

Recurring occurrence identity and mutation receipts must remain stable under retries, exclusions and rescheduling. Series edits must preserve existing individually edited occurrence IDs and metadata; requested-range projection includes retained exceptions even if the new cadence no longer generates their original slot. Multiple distinct meetings at the same time are allowed. These are compatibility constraints for the implementation specs, not an invitation to infer attendance, duplicate seed rows, or silently discard exceptions.
